Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Hone ("we", "us", or "our") operates gethone.co and sells digital products. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when making a purchase:

  • Purchase information: your email address, name, and payment details. Payment details are processed directly by our payment processor (Lemon Squeezy) and are never stored on our servers.
  • Communications: if you contact us, we retain that correspondence.

We also collect limited technical information automatically:

  • Browser type, operating system, and device type
  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Referring URL and IP address (anonymized where possible)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver your purchased digital products
  • To send purchase receipts and download links
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve our products and website
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send marketing emails without your explicit consent.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Lemon Squeezy — payment processing and product delivery. See Lemon Squeezy's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Pinterest — social media marketing. See Pinterest's Privacy Policy.
  • Etsy — marketplace sales channel. See Etsy's Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies

Our website uses only cookies necessary for basic functionality. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Third-party services may set their own cookies governed by their policies.

5. Data Retention

We retain purchase records as required by law (typically 7 years for financial records). Support communications are retained for 2 years. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
